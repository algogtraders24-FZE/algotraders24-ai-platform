# Q0.7 Parity Model

## compareParity() (Q0.7.49/50)

`compareParity(left, right)` (`runtime/strategy-ir/parity-engine.ts`) compares two `StrategyIR`s translated from (presumably) equivalent source strategies on two platforms, feature-by-feature. Every difference is reported explicitly — a `ParityReport` with zero `differences` is the only honest way to claim two platforms' strategies are equivalent, matching Q0.7.49's own instruction verbatim. Fields compared: `indicators` (DATA_DIFFERENCE), `conditions`/`entries`/`exits`/`risk`/`positionManagement`/`execution` (EXECUTION_DIFFERENCE, except `conditions` which is SEMANTIC_PARITY), `barCloseSemantics`/`repaintingModel`/`timezone`/`session` (PLATFORM_DIFFERENCE), plus an explicit `unsupportedSemantics` check (UNSUPPORTED) whenever either side carries a BLOCKING `UnsupportedSemantic`.

## Classification (Q0.7.50)

`ParityDifferenceCategory`: `EXACT_PARITY|SEMANTIC_PARITY|EXECUTION_DIFFERENCE|DATA_DIFFERENCE|PLATFORM_DIFFERENCE|UNSUPPORTED|UNKNOWN`. Categorization is a deterministic, fixed mapping from WHICH FIELD differed — not a similarity score, and not something a future sprint should turn into one without a very deliberate design decision (Q0.4's own explicit "Execution Reality Grade, Statistical Robustness, and Strategy Quality remain three permanently separate concepts, never merged into one score" rule extends naturally to parity classification too).

## Trades matched positionally, deliberately

Fields are compared via a canonicalized structural hash (`computeCanonicalHash({v: value})`, wrapped so an `undefined` optional field like `session` is still hashable rather than throwing) — there is no fuzzy/similarity matching anywhere in this engine. `test/strategy-ir-parity.test.ts` proves: an IR compared against an exact clone of itself is `identical: true` with zero differences; MQL5-netting vs. MT5-hedging fixtures produce an `EXECUTION_DIFFERENCE` on `positionManagement` (the account-mode divergence, never hidden); every reported difference carries a valid category and non-empty `leftValue`/`rightValue`; and the comparison is symmetric in WHICH features it flags (though `leftIrHash`/`rightIrHash`/deltas are direction-sensitive, per `test/fidelity-comparison.test.ts`'s established precedent for the analogous `FidelityComparison` engine in Q0.6).

## `identical` (whole-result) vs. `differences` (per-feature) — deliberately two different questions

`report.identical` is `leftIrHash === rightIrHash` — a strict, whole-IR equality check that will almost always be `false` across genuinely different platforms (since `sourcePlatform`/`sourceHash`/`provenance` participate in `computeCanonicalIRHash`, per `docs/Q0.7_TRANSLATION_CONTRACT.md`). `report.differences` is the finer-grained, per-feature answer to "but is the TRADING LOGIC actually different" — a `ParityReport` with `identical: false` and zero `differences` is a legitimate, meaningful result: two IRs from different platforms whose EXECUTABLE semantics are provably identical, differing only in origin metadata.

## What Q0.7 does NOT do

No automatic "which platform's translation is more accurate" verdict — `ParityReport` reports WHAT differed, never which side is "right" (that judgment requires ground truth, out of scope, deferred to a future `PlatformDiscrepancyReport`-style sprint per `docs/Q0.4_BACKTEST_ARCHITECTURE.md`). No N-way comparison (pairwise only). No persistence layer for parity results.
