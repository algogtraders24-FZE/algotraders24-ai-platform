## What was genuinely new and required

1. **`PendingOrderManagementCallSite`** and its supporting types (`domain/mql-importer/semantic-model.ts`) — no existing site type distinguishes `OrderModify` from `PositionModify` or `OrderDelete` from `PositionClose`; Q0.8's `ModifyCallSite`/`PartialCloseCallSite` only ever classified generically.
2. **`PendingOrderManagementPolicy`** (`domain/pending-order-management-policy.ts`) — the strategy-INTENT layer Q0.13.4 requires, deliberately distinct from `OrderModificationIntent` (execution-layer) and from `PositionManagementPolicy` (a different subject: open positions, not pending orders).
3. **`PendingOrderManagementIR`** (`domain/strategy-ir/pending-order-management-ir.ts`) — the IR-layer mirror, field-for-field identical to the Policy shape by design (never two independently-evolving shapes).
4. **The conditional/unconditional detector** (`detectPendingOrderManagementConditional`/`recordUnconditionalPendingManagementIfNeeded`, `runtime/mql-importer/semantic-analyzer.ts`) — genuinely new logic; nothing in Q0.8-Q0.12 preserves a modification's guarding condition as a structured shape.
5. **The runtime evaluator** (`runtime/simulation/pending-order-management.ts`) — genuinely new; nothing previously evaluated a policy against a live pending order each bar.

## A real pre-existing gap discovered and fixed (rule 15)

`ir-generator.ts`'s `MANAGEMENT_ACCESSOR_FUNCTIONS` set (Q0.10) carved `OrderOpenPrice`/`OrderLots`/`OrderStopLoss`/`OrderTakeProfit` out of the "gating position query" heuristic that flips `positionManagement.pyramiding`/`reversal` to the conservative (execution-BLOCKING) branch. `OrderType()` — a single-value accessor with the exact same shape (reads one field of an already-selected order, no iteration/selection of its own) — was missing from that set. This meant Q0.13's own canonical pattern (`if(OrderType()==OP_BUYSTOP) OrderDelete(ticket)`) unconditionally forced the conservative branch and blocked the ENTIRE strategy via `checkReductionEligibility`'s unrelated pyramiding/reversal checks, for a reason structurally unconnected to pending-order management. Fixed by adding `"OrderType"` to the set — purely additive, verified safe by re-running the full 907-test baseline (zero regressions) and confirming G01 (which also calls `OrderType()`, among many genuinely-gating calls) remains correctly `BLOCKED` (it still has `PositionsTotal`/`PositionGetTicket` etc. keeping `gatingPositionQueries.length > 0`).

A second, narrower pre-existing gap (also fixed additively): `PositionClose()` had no `detectCall` dispatch case at all — a bare, unguarded `PositionClose(ticket)` fell through to `UNRESOLVED_CROSS_FILE_CALL`. Now recognized and recorded distinctly (never confused with `OrderDelete`).

## Why `eligibility-gate.ts` was deliberately left untouched

Q0.13.15 ("Management behavior must affect compilation eligibility") is satisfied at the `ir-generator.ts` layer instead: an unprovable rule is recorded with `markUnsupported(..., "WARNING", ...)`. `WARNING`, not `BLOCKING`, is deliberate — `validateStrategyIR()`'s own frozen Q0.7.31 rule ("any BLOCKING UnsupportedSemantic makes the whole IR execution-ineligible") would otherwise fail the ENTIRE strategy for one unresolved management rule, exactly mirroring Q0.10's own precedent for its breakeven/trailing/partialClose/maxHoldingPeriod caveats (all WARNING-severity). The actual execution-blocking mechanism for an individual rule is `executableRules()` (`domain/pending-order-management-policy.ts`) — a rule that fails its provability gate is simply never handed to the runtime evaluator, which is the correct, narrowly-scoped BLOCKED outcome Q0.13 asks for.
