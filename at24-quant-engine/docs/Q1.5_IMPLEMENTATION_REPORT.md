## A genuine architecture contradiction found and corrected mid-sprint

Before implementing `maxEntries`, runtime tracing of `decision-builder.ts` proved the audit-stage assumption wrong: `docs/Q1.4_FUTURE_PLATFORM_CONTRACT_CHECK.md`'s claim that "unlimited accumulation actually happens" was inaccurate — pyramiding was **completely unreachable** through the normal decision pipeline pre-Q1.5, not merely uncapped. This was reported, the user explicitly authorized proceeding with the corrected understanding, and Q1.5.4 made pyramiding genuinely reachable for the first time (gated behind `allowPyramiding: true`) rather than merely adding a cap to something that already worked. Full detail and the corrected historical statement: `docs/Q1.5_PYRAMIDING_POLICY.md`.

## Constraints honored
