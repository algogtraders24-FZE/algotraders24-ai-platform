# Q0.9 Parity

Extends Q0.8's `computeCrossPlatformSemanticHash`/`compareParity` concept (IR-level MQL4-vs-MQL5 comparison) one layer further, to the **compiled StrategySpec** and **simulated result** level. Tested in `test/q09-parity.test.ts` and cross-referenced by `test/q09-failure-catalog.test.ts` item 22.

## The fixture pair

`test/fixtures/q09-mql-e2e-fixtures.ts` defines the same EMA(9)/EMA(21) crossover + direction-symmetric fixed-distance SL/TP strategy in both dialects:

- MQL5: `CTrade.Buy`/`CTrade.Sell`, `input` parameters, `iMA(...,MODE_EMA,...)`.
- MQL4: `OrderSend(OP_BUY/OP_SELL,...)`, `extern` parameters, the same `iMA` call shape.

## What is asserted to MATCH across dialects

- Both reduce successfully (`strategySpec` present, no BLOCKING errors).
- Identical `risk.stopLoss`/`risk.takeProfit` (`{type:"fixed-distance", distance: 0.005}` / `{..., distance: 0.01}`) — the arithmetic reconstruction is dialect-agnostic by construction (`resolveRiskLegBinding` works on the AST, not on surface syntax).
- Identical entry directions and identical resolved `ir.indicators` (`EMA(9)`, `EMA(21)`) — same families, same params, regardless of `iMA` being called via `_Symbol`/`Symbol()` or `CTrade`/`OrderSend`.
- Both, run through the same synthetic bars, produce a `CompilationSimulationResult` with a valid `simulationResultHash` — the simulation bridge itself is dialect-agnostic (it only ever sees a `StrategySpec`, never raw MQL).

## What is asserted to correctly DIVERGE (never forced to match)

- `sourcePlatform` (`MT4_MQL4` vs `MT5_MQL5`) and `positionManagement.accountingMode` — MQL5/CTrade defaults to netting, MQL4/OrderSend is hedging-capable by platform default. This is a REAL, disclosed platform divergence (each dialect gets its own honest WARNING-level `UnsupportedSemantic` note per `docs/Q0.9_SEMANTIC_LOSS.md`'s "assume platform default" rule) — parity here means "both are individually correct for their own platform," never "force them to report the same account mode."
- A deliberately-introduced difference (a different SL distance in one dialect's source) IS correctly detected as a risk mismatch — proving the parity comparison has real discriminating power and isn't a rubber stamp that would pass any two inputs.

## What parity does NOT claim

Trade-for-trade fill identity across dialects is not asserted and is not expected: Q0.5's simulation engine operates purely on the compiled `StrategySpec` plus bar data, so two StrategySpecs with identical risk/entry semantics WILL simulate identically given identical bars — this is a structural consequence of the engine being deterministic and dialect-blind (Q0.5.29), not something Q0.9 needs to separately re-verify at the trade level once `risk`/`entryRules`/`indicators` are already proven equal.
