# Q0.3 Risk Architecture

## Q0.3.1 — Audit of existing contracts and where Risk Evaluation sits

Read before implementation: `RiskSpecification` (Q0.2, extended in Q0.2's own sprint — sizing, stop/target, breakeven/trailing/partial-close/session-hours/holding-period/simultaneous-positions/daily-loss, all **specification-only, no evaluation logic**), `ExecutionSpecification` (fill/cost/latency assumptions, also specification-only), `OrderIntent` (Q0 — "what the strategy wants executed," references a `Decision`), `Position` (Q0 — resulting exposure, `OPEN`/`CLOSED`), `Decision` (Q0 — action + reasoning, references a `Signal`), `MarketState` (Q0/Q0.2 — evaluation-time snapshot), `StrategySpec` (Q0 — carries `RiskSpecification`/`ExecutionSpecification` as nested fields).

**The gap Q0.3 fills**: nothing between "here is a `RiskSpecification`" (policy) and "here is an `OrderIntent`/`Position`" (result) evaluated that policy against anything. Q0.2 built the contract; Q0.3 builds the deterministic function that reads it.

### Four distinct concepts (Q0.3.1's required distinction)

```
RiskSpecification   = configuration / policy
                       (Q0.2: what the strategy WANTS — sizing method, stop/target rules,
                       breakeven/trailing/partial-close triggers, session windows, limits)
        |
        v
RiskEvaluation       = deterministic evaluation of policy against state
                       (Q0.3: pure function — RiskEvaluationInput -> RiskEvaluationResult.
                       Reads a RiskSpecification + current market/portfolio state,
                       decides what SHOULD happen. Never executes anything.)
        |
        v
RiskDecision         = the result of that evaluation
                       (Q0.3: RiskEvaluationResult — outcome + action + violations.
                       An instruction, not an executed fact.)
        |
        v
Execution            = actual simulation of order behavior
                       (NOT built in Q0.3 — a future layer that takes a RiskAction like
                       MOVE_STOP or PARTIAL_CLOSE and actually simulates the fill,
                       updates a Position, applies slippage/spread/commission from
                       ExecutionSpecification. Q0.3 produces instructions only.)
```

`evaluateRisk()` (`src/runtime/risk/pipeline.ts`) is the single entry point. It answers exactly one question, in one of two modes selected by which optional field of `RiskEvaluationInput` is populated:

- **ENTRY mode** (`proposedEntry` set): "given this proposed trade and current portfolio/daily-loss state, is this entry allowed?"
- **MANAGEMENT mode** (`existingPosition` set): "given this open position and current market state, what (if anything) should happen to its risk parameters?"

Both modes share one pipeline and one result shape because a future backtester needs to call the same function every bar for both questions — see `docs/AT24_QUANT_ENGINE_MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md` for how this composes with the rest of the engine.

## Why Risk Evaluation must not execute orders

Execution requires broker-specific fill logic, slippage/spread simulation, and Position mutation — all explicitly out of scope for Q0.3 (Q0.3.23) and all belonging to a distinct future layer that Q0.2's `reality-models.ts` interfaces (`FillModel`, `SlippageModel`, etc.) already anticipate. Keeping Risk Evaluation execution-free is what makes it reusable by a future Backtest Engine, Research Engine, Monte Carlo engine, and Portfolio Engine simultaneously (Q0's "ultimate success criterion") — none of those consumers would be able to reuse it if it hard-coded one execution model.

## Trade Risk vs. Position Risk vs. Portfolio Risk (Q0.3.22)

| Layer | Scope | Built in Q0.3? |
|---|---|---|
| **Trade Risk** | the risk of ONE proposed trade in isolation — stop distance, R-multiple, entry geometry | Yes (`geometry.ts`, `r-multiple.ts`) |
| **Position Risk** | the risk of ONE open position over its lifetime — breakeven/trailing/partial-close/holding-period | Yes (`breakeven.ts`, `trailing.ts`, `partial-close.ts`, `holding-period.ts`) |
| **Portfolio Risk** | aggregate risk across ALL open positions/strategies — total/symbol/strategy/correlated exposure, portfolio drawdown, risk budget | **No** — only future interfaces reserved (`domain/portfolio-risk-boundary.ts`) |

`maxSimultaneousPositions` and `dailyLossLimit` sit at the Trade-Risk-gating-the-portfolio boundary: they read a portfolio-level COUNT/loss figure the caller supplies, but do not themselves compute exposure, correlation, or drawdown — those remain Portfolio Risk, deferred.
