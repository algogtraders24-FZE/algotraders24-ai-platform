## Two additional structural checks

- `validatePendingOrderManagementPolicy` rejects a duplicate rule id (mirrors every other domain validator's own duplicate-id check).
- D1 (`runSimulation`) and D2/D3 (`runMultiFidelitySimulation`) produce the IDENTICAL `ordersCancelled` count for the identical policy — the mirror is behaviorally proven, not merely code-reviewed.

## A design choice this catalog makes explicit

Item 13 is the clearest illustration of Q0.13's own architectural judgment call (documented in full in `Q0.13_EXISTING_ARCHITECTURE_AUDIT.md`/`Q0.13_COMPILATION.md`): an individual unresolved pending-order-management RULE is blocked from execution — the STRATEGY as a whole is not, unless its own entry/exit logic is independently unresolved. This mirrors Q0.10's own established precedent exactly, and is proven directly (not merely asserted) by test #13's use of a strategy with BOTH a fully valid, resolvable entry AND one unresolvable management rule.
