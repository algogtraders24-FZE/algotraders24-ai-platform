# Q0.4 Backtest Failure Catalog

Q0.4.41: the 24 failure modes named in the sprint brief, each with what it is, why it produces a misleading result, and AT24's documented stance. No implementation in Q0.4 — several of these are already partially addressed by earlier sprints (noted where true); the rest are design commitments for future sprints.

| # | Failure mode | What it is | Why it misleads | AT24 stance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lookahead** | strategy logic reads data that would not have been available at the simulated decision time | inflates performance arbitrarily — the strategy is "cheating" by seeing the future | Structurally prevented for indicators by Q0.2's incremental step-function design + no-lookahead test; `TimeFrontier` (Q0.2) enforces it for raw bar access. A future simulation core must extend the same guarantee to cross-symbol/cross-timeframe reads (Q0.4.27/28). |
| 2 | **Repainting** | a value displayed/used at time T changes when recomputed later with more data available | a strategy that looks profitable using repainted values would not have made the same decisions live | See `Q0.4_LOOKAHEAD_REPAINTING.md` for full treatment; Pine's documented causes (Q0.4.2) are the primary researched reference. |
| 3 | **Intrabar ambiguity** | OHLC alone cannot order same-bar SL/TP or multiple same-bar events | silently picking the favorable order inflates results | Full treatment in `Q0.4_INTRABAR_ENGINE.md` — mandatory, never silently favorable. |
| 4 | **Synthetic data** | executing against Heikin Ashi/Renko/Range/Kagi/etc. prices as if they were real fills | those prices were never actually tradeable | `Q0.4_INTRABAR_ENGINE.md`'s Synthetic Data Detection — `REAL_MARKET_PRICE` vs `SYNTHETIC_PRICE` classification, mandatory warning. |
| 5 | **Missing tick** | gaps in historical tick data silently filled or skipped | a fill resolved against reconstructed/interpolated ticks is presented as if it were real tick evidence | Must be represented as reduced `DataFidelity` (falls back from D4/D5 toward D2/D3), never silently upgraded to "tick-verified." |
| 6 | **Missing bid/ask** | spread-dependent fills computed without real bid/ask data | assumes a spread that may not have existed at that instant | Forces `DataFidelity` ceiling of D4 (trade-tick) or lower even if trade ticks exist — D5 requires the bid/ask itself, not just trades. |
| 7 | **Timestamp disorder** | events processed out of chronological order | causes phantom lookahead (an "earlier" decision sees a "later" event's effect) | Prevented by Q0.2's `validateMarketSeries` (chronological-order check) and Q0.4.5's `(timestamp, sequence)` total-order event queue. |
| 8 | **Duplicate data** | the same observation counted twice | double-counts a price move, inflates or deflates volatility/volume-derived metrics | Prevented by Q0.2's `validateMarketSeries` duplicate-timestamp detection. |
| 9 | **Timezone mismatch** | two data sources or a session definition disagree on what timezone a timestamp is in | a session/holiday check silently misfires, or two symbols appear synchronized when they aren't | Q0.3's session evaluation already uses explicit IANA-timezone `Intl.DateTimeFormat` conversion, never machine-local time — same discipline must extend to any future calendar/session engine (Q0.4.29). |
| 10 | **DST mismatch** | a session boundary computed before/after a daylight-saving transition without accounting for it | a session that should be "open" reads as "closed" or vice versa for one or two days a year | `Intl.DateTimeFormat`-based conversion (already Q0.3's approach) inherently handles DST correctly for a given IANA zone — the risk is a future engine bypassing this with manual UTC-offset arithmetic instead; explicitly forbidden. |
| 11 | **Session mismatch** | applying the wrong instrument's/exchange's trading-hours calendar | a strategy is allowed to trade (or blocked from trading) at times the real market was actually closed (or open) | Reserved for the future Session/Calendar Engine (Q0.4.29) — must be instrument-specific, never a single global assumption. |
| 12 | **Gaps** | a genuine price gap (weekend, news, illiquid open) misrepresented as continuous price movement | a stop/limit order's fill price is misestimated (see also #14) | `Q0.4_INTRABAR_ENGINE.md`'s order-type gap-behavior table addresses stop/limit gap-through explicitly. |
| 13 | **Same-bar SL/TP** | both SL and TP fall within one bar's high-low range | ambiguous which was hit first — see full treatment | `Q0.4_INTRABAR_ENGINE.md`, the sprint's flagship mandatory topic. |
| 14 | **Stop gap-through** | price gaps past a stop level without trading at it | filling at the stop price (rather than the worse, gapped-through price) is unrealistically favorable | `Q0.4_EVENT_MODEL.md`'s order-type table: STOP orders must fill at the realistic gapped-through price, never silently at the stop level. |
| 15 | **Limit ambiguity** | whether a limit order actually filled depends on whether price truly traded through the level, not merely touched/closed past it | overstates fill likelihood if "close crossed the level" is treated as sufficient | Q0.4.2's researched Pine rule (limit fills require an actual trade through the level within the bar) is AT24's adopted standard — see `Q0.4_ADOPT_IMPROVE_AVOID_INVENT.md`. |
| 16 | **Partial fills** | an order fills only partially, remainder unfilled/cancelled/still-working | treating a partial fill as a full fill misstates position size and realized exposure | Explicit `PARTIALLY_FILLED` order-lifecycle state (Q0.4.8) — never coerced to `FILLED`. |
| 17 | **Zero spread** | assuming bid == ask | overstates every strategy's edge, since real trading always crosses a spread | Q0.2's `ExecutionSpecification.costsExplicitlyZero` guard already prevents a spread from being silently unset — the same explicitness principle applies to any future spread model (Q0.4.15). |
| 18 | **Zero slippage** | assuming every fill happens at exactly the requested price | overstates edge, especially for larger size or volatile conditions | Same Q0.2 guard covers slippage; future stochastic slippage models must use a fixed, documented seed (Q0.4.16) rather than genuinely random behavior, so re-running is still deterministic. |
| 19 | **Zero latency** | assuming instantaneous order submission-to-fill | overstates edge for any strategy sensitive to fast-moving prices | Reserved `LatencyModel` (Q0.4.18) — `ZERO` remains a legitimate, explicit choice, just never an unlabeled default. |
| 20 | **Incorrect margin** | margin/leverage simulation diverges from the real broker's rules | can allow a backtest to take positions a live account could never actually hold | Reserved `MarginModel`/Buying-Power concepts (Q0.4.19), explicitly kept separate from `RiskSpecification` per the sprint's own instruction. |
| 21 | **Incorrect settlement** | ignoring settlement delays (e.g. T+1/T+2 for some asset classes) | can allow using proceeds from a sale before they were actually available, inflating apparent capital efficiency | Reserved `SettlementModel` (Q0.4.20) — interface only in Q0.4. |
| 22 | **Source translation mismatch** | a strategy translated from MQL/Pine/etc. behaves differently in AT24 than the original due to a translation error or an unavoidable semantic gap | the AT24 backtest result no longer represents the strategy the author actually intended | This is exactly what Source Fidelity grading (Q0.4.34) and `PlatformDiscrepancyReport` (Q0.4.33) exist to catch and disclose, not silently absorb. |
| 23 | **Stale quotes** | using a quote that was no longer current at the simulated instant (e.g. a feed outage papered over with the last-known price) | a fill computed against a stale price doesn't reflect what the market actually offered | Must be representable as a data-quality gap (relates to #5/#6), never silently forward-filled — see Q0.4.28's explicit "never silently forward-fill unavailable information" rule. |
| 24 | **Insufficient liquidity** | assuming an order of any size fills fully at the quoted price | overstates results for larger position sizes relative to real available liquidity | Reserved `LiquidityModel`/`MarketImpactModel` (Q0.4.22/23) — interface only, no implementation planned near-term. |

## What Q0.4 does NOT do

No detector, validator, or runtime check is implemented in `src/` for any of the 24 items above. This catalog is the checklist a future Q0.5+ simulation core's own test suite must be measured against — a new execution feature that doesn't address (or explicitly, visibly defer) at least the item(s) it's directly relevant to has not met this sprint's bar.
