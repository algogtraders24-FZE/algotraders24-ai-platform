# M0.1 — Product Model Freeze

**Status:** FROZEN — decision document only, no code, no schema. This is the contract M1 (TradingSystem Schema) and everything downstream must build from. If a future sprint needs to violate a decision here, that violation must come back to this document and be revised explicitly — not silently coded around.

**Input:** [M0_marketplace_research.md](M0_marketplace_research.md) — the incumbent research confirmed AT24's original thesis rather than changing it. No platform surveyed (MQL5 Market/Signals, Collective2, Darwinex, TradingView, cTrader) does backtest audit + forced out-of-sample/walk-forward + curve-fit screening + a unified health score + an explainable trust badge, applied uniformly at the point a product is listed.

**One-line product definition:**
> AT24 Marketplace = Trading System + Evidence + Independent Validation + Risk + History + Explainable Trust

---

## 1. The 12 frozen principles

1. **Seller claims are never treated as evidence by default.** A number a seller types in is a claim, not a fact, until AT24's pipeline reproduces or verifies it. (Directly answers MQL5 Market's core failure: unaudited seller screenshots as the only "performance information.")
2. **Backtests must become reproducible/verified evidence, not screenshots.** A backtest is only evidence if AT24 (or an AT24-controlled process) can regenerate it from disclosed inputs and get the same result.
3. **OOS / walk-forward validation is part of AT24 validation, not an optional premium feature.** Every system that wants any verified trust status goes through it — it is not a paid upsell.
4. **Curve-fit/robustness testing is mandatory for a meaningful verification level.** A system that hasn't been perturbation/regime-tested cannot claim a verification level above the most basic ("submitted, unverified") tier.
5. **Live/paper evidence is separate from historical evidence.** A backtest and a live track record are different evidence classes with different trust weight — never merged into one number that hides which kind backs a claim.
6. **Risk-adjusted performance matters more than raw return/profit.** Ranking, scoring, and headline display default to risk-adjusted terms (drawdown, Sharpe/Sortino-class measures, recovery factor), not net profit or return %.
7. **Every important performance number needs provenance.** Data source, broker, symbol, period, timeframe, spread/commission/swap model, execution assumptions — no metric is displayed without the conditions that produced it traveling with it.
8. **Every verification result needs an audit trail explaining why it passed/failed.** Not just a pass/fail flag — a record of what was checked, the thresholds used, and the actual values, inspectable by the seller and (in some form) the buyer.
9. **Marketplace ranking must not simply rank by profit.** Default sort/rank is a function of the frozen score (§3), not raw return — this is a direct rejection of MQL5's rating-by-popularity and Collective2's return-first leaderboard framing.
10. **A strategy version and its evidence must remain linked permanently.** Evidence never migrates or gets reattributed across versions. A new version starts with zero evidence and must earn its own — this was already true in G01's own version discipline (v0.1 vs v0.2 kept strictly separate) and is now a marketplace-wide rule, not just a research-hygiene habit.
11. **Trust badges are generated by AT24 rules, never seller-controlled.** No seller-settable flag, tier, or self-assigned label influences trust-status computation.
12. **The architecture must support third-party systems later, even though the first marketplace systems may be AT24-owned.** Nothing in the schema, evidence model, or validation pipeline may hardcode an "AT24 is the seller" assumption. Gold Auto Strategy is system #1 through this pipeline, not a special case of it.

## 2. Core architecture chain (revised)

The original M-series sketch implied `TradingSystem → Score`. That is rejected. The frozen chain is:

```
TradingSystem
      ↓
Evidence
      ↓
Validation
      ↓
Risk Analysis
      ↓
History
      ↓
Trust Status
      ↓
Score
```

**Why this ordering, and why it matters later:** the Score is an *interpretation* of everything above it, not a primary fact. Evidence, Validation, Risk Analysis, and History must each be independently inspectable and independently correct on their own terms — a buyer, an auditor, or AT24 itself must be able to stop at any layer and ask "is this layer trustworthy?" without needing the Score to answer that. This separation is what makes principle 8 (explainable audit trail) possible at all, and it is what will matter most once third-party traders (principle 12) start submitting systems whose Evidence AT24 did not generate itself — the pipeline must be able to say precisely which layer a submission failed at, not just emit a low score.

## 3. Frozen definitions

### What is a Trading System?
The versioned, identity-bearing object representing one strategy (logic + parameters + target instruments/timeframes/execution model), independent of any specific backtest or live run. A Trading System owns zero performance data itself — all performance lives one layer down, in Evidence, scoped to a specific Version. (Gold Auto Strategy is one Trading System; v0.1 and v0.2 are two of its Versions, per G01's existing version history.)

### What is Evidence?
Any structured, provenance-tagged performance record produced by a defined process — a backtest run, a trade-level export, a live/paper account feed — that is **not manually editable** after generation (principle 2). Evidence is always one of two classes (principle 5): **Historical Evidence** (backtest/simulated) or **Live Evidence** (paper or real-money, platform-observed). Evidence always carries provenance (principle 7) and is always bound to exactly one Trading System Version (principle 10).

### What is a Validation?
The record of running Evidence through one or more of AT24's defined check layers (data integrity, statistical validity, OOS/walk-forward, parameter robustness, regime testing — per the M-series M4/M5 sprints) and recording pass/fail plus the actual measured values and thresholds used (principle 8). A Validation is always run against a specific piece of Evidence, never against a seller's narrative description of that evidence.

### What is a Version?
An immutable snapshot of a Trading System's logic/parameters at a point in time, identified by a version string (v1.0, v1.1, v2.0, ...). A new Version starts with zero Evidence, zero Validation, and zero Trust Status of its own (principle 10) — nothing carries forward automatically, even a minor version bump.

### What is a Trust Status?
An AT24-computed classification (e.g., UNVERIFIED → BACKTEST VERIFIED → ROBUSTNESS VERIFIED → PAPER VERIFIED → LIVE VERIFIED → AT24 VERIFIED, per the M7 sprint) derived purely from which Validation layers a given Version's Evidence has passed. It is a rules output, never a field a seller can set (principle 11), and it is always scoped to one Version, not the Trading System as a whole (a system can have v1.0 at LIVE VERIFIED and v2.0 back at UNVERIFIED).

### What is a Marketplace Listing?
The publishable, buyer-facing object that references one Trading System Version and surfaces its Trust Status, Score, and Evidence summary. A Listing cannot exist without a Version, and a Listing's displayed performance data is always a read-only projection of that Version's Evidence — never independently editable listing copy for performance claims.

### What is an AT24 Score?
The computed, single-number-or-small-vector interpretation sitting at the *end* of the chain in §2 — a function of Validation results, Risk Analysis, and History, not a restatement of raw profit or return (principle 6, principle 9). The Score exists to make systems comparable at a glance; it is explicitly not the source of truth (the layers above it are), and its formula is AT24-owned, not seller-influenced (principle 11).

### What can a seller claim?
A seller may author: system name, description, category, supported instruments/timeframes, pricing, documentation, screenshots/media, and the strategy narrative. A seller may **not** directly author: any performance metric, drawdown figure, win rate, profit factor, or similar number that appears anywhere near a Trust Status or Score — those fields are always Evidence-derived and AT24-computed (principle 1). This is the single largest and most deliberate divergence from MQL5 Market, where the entire performance narrative on a listing is seller-authored and unaudited.

### What must AT24 independently verify?
At minimum, for any Version to carry a Trust Status above UNVERIFIED: data integrity of the Evidence's source, statistical validity (sample size/trade count sufficiency), out-of-sample/walk-forward performance, and parameter-robustness/anti-curve-fit behavior. AT24 does not need to independently re-run every backtest byte-for-byte at MVP, but it must independently verify the *inputs* (data provenance) and *process* (that the claimed validation layers were actually executed against real Evidence, not asserted).

### What makes a system eligible for publication?
A Listing may go to `SUBMITTED` with only an identity-complete Trading System Version (no Evidence required to draft a listing). It may **not** progress past `UNDER_REVIEW` toward `PUBLISHED` (per the M9 publication-state machine) without: at least one class of Evidence attached, at least a data-integrity + statistical-validity Validation pass, and an assigned Trust Status of at least the lowest verified tier (BACKTEST VERIFIED). A Version with only seller narrative and zero Evidence is permanently blocked at `REJECTED` or held in `DRAFT`, never `PUBLISHED`.

## 4. Explicitly deferred (not part of this freeze)

- Seller economy / pricing mechanics (M13) — stays deferred until after the validation architecture exists, per the original spec's own ordering.
- Exact numeric thresholds for each Validation layer (min trade count, OOS split ratio, robustness perturbation ranges, etc.) — these are M4/M5 implementation details, not product-model decisions, and can be tuned later without breaking this contract.
- Exact Score formula/weights (M6) — only the *shape* (interpretation-of-evidence, not primary fact) is frozen here, not the math.
- UI/UX for any of this — out of scope for a product-model document.
