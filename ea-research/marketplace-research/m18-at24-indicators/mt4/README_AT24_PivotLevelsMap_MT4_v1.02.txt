AT24 PIVOT LEVELS MAP - MetaTrader 4 version v1.02 (licensed utility EA)

WHAT IT SHOWS
- Daily and weekly pivot levels (P, R1, R2, S1, S2); monthly pivots, R3/S3 and Camarilla H3/H4/L3/L4 can be switched on.
- The daily CPR (top and bottom) and its width compared with the last 20 days: NARROW, WIDE or normal - versus the average only, never a forecast.
- Where price is now (above, inside or below the CPR), the nearest level above and below with the distance, and a table of every level with the nearest one highlighted. Labels with prices on the chart.

INSTALL (MetaTrader 4) - this product is a licensed UTILITY EXPERT ADVISOR
It draws on your chart and never places or modifies orders. It is an Expert Advisor only because MetaTrader does not let indicators check a licence online.
1. In MT4 choose File > Open Data Folder, open MQL4 > Experts and copy AT24_PivotLevelsMap_Licensed.ex4 into it.
2. Restart MT4 or right-click Expert Advisors in the Navigator > Refresh.
3. Allow the licence check once per terminal: Tools > Options > Expert Advisors > tick "Allow WebRequest for listed URL" and add https://www.algotraders24.ai
4. Drag AT24_PivotLevelsMap_Licensed from Navigator > Expert Advisors onto a chart (any symbol). On the Inputs tab paste your licence key into "InpLicenseKey" (AT24 dashboard: Dashboard > Purchases > your product).
5. Keep the AutoTrading button switched on so the EA can run. It does not trade. The dashboard appears top-left.

LICENCE
