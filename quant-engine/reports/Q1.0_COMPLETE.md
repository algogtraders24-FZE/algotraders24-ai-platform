## 6. Data-quality findings

The most significant finding of this sprint: **BTCUSD_EXNESS and XAUUSD_ZS_EXNESS have serious real data gaps**, not merely normal weekend closures. BTCUSD_EXNESS is missing over 60% of its nominal 2.6-year date range (gaps of 155, 216, 74, and 13 days). XAUUSD_ZS_EXNESS has 38-40 anomalous gaps including a 130-day hole and multiple multi-week weekly-only stretches. XAUUSD_EXNESS (the flagship, most-proven symbol) also has one real 169-day gap plus a ~6-week weekly-only stretch in Nov-Dec 2024 that happens to fall inside the Q0.6/Q0.9 MACD baseline's test window — confirmed, by direct ledger inspection, to coincidentally contain zero of that baseline's 208 trades, so it did not distort that specific result, but this was luck, not protection: `execution_mtf.py` has no gap-awareness and will silently trade through a real data hole. EURUSD_EXNESS, GBPUSD_EXNESS, and USOIL_EXNESS are comparatively clean (≤14-day max gaps). Full detail: `Q1.0_DATA_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md`.

Given this, BTCUSD_EXNESS and XAUUSD_ZS_EXNESS are classified **CONDITIONALLY_SUPPORTED** rather than SUPPORTED — the engine works correctly on them (real trades, fully deterministic), but they now carry a standing UI disclosure plus a per-request warning when a chosen date range overlaps a specific known gap.

## 7. Date-range behavior

`checkDataCoverage()` now distinguishes three cases: fully within range (accepted, plus a gap warning if applicable), no overlap at all (`DATA_UNAVAILABLE`, "does not overlap the available historical data"), and partial overlap (`DATA_UNAVAILABLE`, message states the actual available range and asks the user to adjust — **never silently truncated**). A new gap-warning system (`data/quant-lite-known-gaps.ts`) surfaces a specific, real, located-gap disclosure whenever a request touches one, in both the Builder and Backtest Setup screens — verified live in the browser (selecting BTCUSD_EXNESS immediately shows its real gap disclosure).

## 20. Known limitations

- The gap registry (`data/quant-lite-known-gaps.ts`) is a static, point-in-time snapshot of this sprint's audit — it will go stale as `market.db` grows. Re-running `q10_gap_audit.py` periodically and updating the registry is a manual step, not automated here.
- Timeframe compatibility was proven on one representative symbol (XAUUSD_EXNESS) and generalized across the other 5 on the documented reasoning that `execution_mtf.py` is symbol-agnostic — not every one of the 42 possible symbol×timeframe combinations was individually live-tested.
- BTCUSD_EXNESS and XAUUSD_ZS_EXNESS remain CONDITIONALLY_SUPPORTED with real, substantial data gaps; a user requesting a long date range on either will get a warning but the choice to proceed is still theirs.
- No performance ceiling was found in this sprint's testing, but only representative (not maximal) window sizes were tested per timeframe — a full-history 1m backtest (2+ years, ~600K-960K rows) was not attempted and could take meaningfully longer than the 2-week window tested here.
- The sprint brief referenced a canonical engine path `quant-engine/quant_engine/execution_mtf.py`, which does not exist — the real canonical path, used consistently since Q0.5, is `quant-engine/spec_engine/execution_mtf.py`. Treated as a naming discrepancy in the brief, not acted on literally.
