## 1. USOIL finding

**RESOLVED.** Built a diagnostic (`quant-engine/scripts/q12_usoil_investigation.py`) that tested a specific hypothesis directly against real data rather than guessing: coarser bars "absorb" (paper over) short real outages visible in finer series, because a 4h/1d bar only needs one real tick anywhere in its window to exist. Confirmed with hard evidence: 370 of 541 USOIL_EXNESS 1h gaps (68%, 73.6% of missing-days) have a 4h bar starting inside them, and every spot-checked covering bar has genuine, non-flat OHLC. The *remaining* residual gap was traced to a second, distinct, strongly-evidenced cause: **510 of 512 short (1-4h) USOIL_EXNESS gaps start at 20:00-22:00 UTC, spanning 509 distinct days (~74% of eligible trading days)** — a genuine daily rollover specific to commodities/metals (XAUUSD/XAUUSD_ZS show the same pattern; EURUSD/GBPUSD/BTCUSD show only 6-8 such gaps total). Reclassified as `EXPECTED_SESSION_BREAK` (new gap type, `AUDIT_RULE_VERSION` bumped `q1.1-gap-rules-v1`→`q1.2-gap-rules-v2`). Full writeup: `Q1.2_USOIL_COVERAGE_INVESTIGATION.md`.

## 3. Market-closure policy

Hybrid (Option E): `KNOWN_MARKET_CLOSURE` (existing weekly-closure rule, renamed only in documentation for clarity) + new `EXPECTED_SESSION_BREAK` (evidence-gated, ~74%+ recurrence required) + `DATA_GAP` (every other classified type). No `UNKNOWN_GAP` bucket was implemented — every gap this sprint's classifier produces already has a specific, evidenced type; a catch-all "unknown" label would either go unused or start absorbing gaps that do have a real classification, which is the opposite of the goal. A full exchange holiday calendar was explicitly **not** built (Stop Condition #7 considered and resolved by documenting the limitation rather than inventing a calendar) — specific holiday-shaped gaps (Dec 24-25, Dec 31-Jan 1) are visible in the data but were not confidently mapped to named holidays, since the evidentiary bar that justified `EXPECTED_SESSION_BREAK` (500+ instances, 74%+ recurrence) wasn't met (only 3-4 instances per symbol). Full policy: `Q1.2_MARKET_CLOSURE_POLICY.md`.
