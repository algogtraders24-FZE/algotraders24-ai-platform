Absolute missing time is nearly identical between timeframes (279.67 vs. 278.0 days) — the ~2.27-point spread is a denominator effect, not a data defect. All 6 major structural gaps (169d, 34d, 28d, 21d, 10d, 6d) have byte-identical boundaries across 1h/4h/1d. Full evidence: `Q1.3_XAUUSD_COVERAGE_DECISION.md`.
