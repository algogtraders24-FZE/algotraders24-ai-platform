## Real gaps found (not inferred — every boundary below was located from actual consecutive-row timestamp jumps)

**BTCUSD_EXNESS** — heavily fragmented. Dense data only in these windows: 2024-01-01→2024-07-29, 2025-01-01→2025-03-15, 2025-10-18 (a single point), 2026-01-01→2026-05-08, 2026-05-22→2026-08-22. Missing entirely: 2024-07-29→2025-01-01 (155 days), 2025-03-15→2025-10-18 (**216 days** — the single largest gap in the database), 2025-10-18→2026-01-01 (74 days), 2026-05-08→2026-05-22 (13 days). Over 60% of its nominal 2024-01-01→2026-08-22 span has no data at all.

**XAUUSD_EXNESS** — mostly dense, with two classes of problem: (1) a run of exact weekly-only bars from 2024-11-18 23:00 to 2025-01-01 23:00 (confirmed by direct inspection: normal hourly density through 2024-11-18 23:00, then exactly one bar per week — Nov 24, Dec 1, Dec 8, Dec 15, Dec 22 — before density presumably resumes; not a market closure, since Gold does not trade one hour per week); (2) a large **169-day** gap from 2025-07-16 to 2026-01-01, plus smaller (3-34 day) gaps scattered through 2025-2026.

**XAUUSD_ZS_EXNESS** — the most gap-affected symbol by count (38-40 anomalous gaps at every timeframe): a similar weekly-only stretch in Oct-Dec 2024, another in Jun-Jul 2025, a **130-day** gap Aug 2025→Jan 2026, and a long run of near-weekly gaps from March through August 2026 (data quality visibly degrades toward the most recent end of its range).

**EURUSD_EXNESS / GBPUSD_EXNESS / USOIL_EXNESS** — clean by comparison: only 1-4 gaps anywhere, all ≤14 days (consistent with a single elongated holiday period, e.g. Christmas/New Year), no repeating weekly-gap pattern.

**Legacy XAUUSD / EURUSD (no `_EXNESS` suffix)** — gaps stay at normal weekend/holiday scale (≤3.3 days); the underlying issue with these two symbols is not gap quality but the complete absence of 1-minute execution data (see Part 2), which makes them unusable by `execution_mtf.py` regardless of how clean their 1h/4h data is.

## Cross-check against the Q0.9 MACD baseline

The Q0.9/Q0.6 canonical MACD result (XAUUSD_EXNESS, 2024-01-01→2024-12-31, 208 trades) runs directly through XAUUSD_EXNESS's weekly-gap window (2024-11-18→2025-01-01). Direct inspection of the real 208-trade ledger confirms **zero trades opened or closed during that window** — the strategy simply had no active signal there, so the gap happened not to distort that particular result. This is coincidental, not protective: a different strategy/date-range combination over the same gap could produce a result that looks complete while silently missing five weeks of real market behavior. **This is the central finding of Part 6** — `execution_mtf.py` has no gap-awareness; it processes whatever rows exist and never flags a hole. See `Q1.0_CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md` for how this affects the CONDITIONALLY_SUPPORTED classification of BTCUSD_EXNESS and XAUUSD_ZS_EXNESS.
