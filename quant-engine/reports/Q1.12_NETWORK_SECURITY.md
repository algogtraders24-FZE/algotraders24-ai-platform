## Failure test, through the real tunnel (Phase 9, reported here since it's a security-adjacent live test)

Service process force-stopped on the VPS. Immediately after: `Invoke-RestMethod -Uri "https://quant-lite-exec.algotraders24.ai/health"` → **`502 Bad Gateway`** — Cloudflare's own edge correctly reports the origin is unreachable, not a fabricated "ok" and not a silent timeout. This is exactly the signature `executionAdapter.ts`'s `runRemote()` already handles (a non-2xx/failed fetch → `ENGINE_UNREACHABLE`), now confirmed to be what the real network path actually produces, not assumed. Service restarted immediately after and confirmed healthy again — no residual state, no orphaned request in flight.

## What remains, honestly

Request-size limiting is the one carried-forward gap from Q1.11 that this sprint didn't close — it's the same low-severity, already-disclosed characteristic (a several-MB junk field would be accepted, not rejected), not new, not hidden, just not prioritized in a sprint whose central objective was the network path itself.
