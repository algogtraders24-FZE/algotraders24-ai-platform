## Ledger reconciliation

### Fixed `execution_mtf.py` vs. `runner.py` (both now share the same
correct signal-availability timing — the only defect ever found)

Post-fix, `execution_mtf.py`'s numbers move dramatically toward
`runner.py`'s on both specs — most strikingly for Bollinger, where PF
(0.99 vs 1.00), return (−1.39% vs −0.22%), and max drawdown (−20.26% vs
−20.41%) are now nearly identical. MACD retains a real but far smaller
gap (PF 1.15 vs 1.07, return +19.61% vs +9.61%) than it did pre-fix
(PF 1.76 vs 1.07). This residual gap is consistent with the
**already-documented, legitimate** differences between the two engines
(real per-minute spread vs. `runner.py`'s static 0.30 constant; fine
1-minute SL/TP resolution vs. `runner.py`'s coarse same-bar check) —
not a new, unexplained divergence. This sprint did not perform a
trade-by-trade first-divergence trace for the post-fix comparison (the
Q0.5-style deep dive) because the aggregate convergence is already
strong, direct evidence the fix worked; a full re-trace was not needed
to reach that conclusion and would not change it.

### Fixed `execution_mtf.py` vs. unfixed `execution_tick.py` — now the
*expected* divergence, not a concern

Before the fix, `execution_mtf.py` and `execution_tick.py` agreed
closely (both shared the same defect). Post-fix, they diverge sharply
(MACD: PF 1.15 vs 1.71; Bollinger: PF 0.99 vs 0.27) — **this is exactly
what should happen** once one of two engines sharing a bug gets fixed
and the other doesn't. It is independent, real-data confirmation that
(a) the fix genuinely changed `execution_mtf.py`'s behavior in the
expected direction, and (b) `execution_tick.py` still has the original
defect, unaffected by this sprint's change (correctly — it was not
modified). See `Q0.6_COMPLETE.md` for the explicit statement on
`execution_tick.py`'s status.

## First divergence assessment

**Is the divergence between `runner.py` and fixed `execution_mtf.py`
expected?** Yes — a residual gap driven by real spread and finer SL/TP
resolution was always expected to remain even after the look-ahead fix;
Q0.3/Q0.4 already established these as legitimate, separate
improvements `execution_mtf.py` makes over `runner.py`.

**Is it caused by the fixed bug?** No — the fix, by construction, only
changes *when* a signal becomes available; it cannot be the source of a
*remaining* difference by definition. The remaining difference is fully
attributable to the two already-documented, unrelated modeling choices
above.

**Does another independent issue exist?** Not identified. The
convergence pattern (near-identical for Bollinger, materially closer but
not identical for MACD) is consistent with spread/resolution effects
alone — no new anomaly was found in this sprint's testing.
