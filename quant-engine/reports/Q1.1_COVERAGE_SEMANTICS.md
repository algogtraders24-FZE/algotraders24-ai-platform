# Q1.1 — Coverage Semantics

## Timeframe consistency (Q1.1.14/15)

Every one of the 7 timeframes per symbol was audited fully independently (own query, own gap detection, own coverage-ratio computation) - no timeframe's result is copied or inferred from another. Real whole-range coverage % per symbol, across all 7 timeframes:

| Symbol | 1m | 5m | 15m | 30m | 1h | 4h | 1d | Spread |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EURUSD_EXNESS | 96.30 | 96.85 | 96.88 | 96.87 | 96.90 | 96.98 | 97.10 | 0.80 |
| GBPUSD_EXNESS | 94.35 | 94.67 | 94.74 | 94.70 | 94.71 | 94.66 | 93.77 | 0.97 |
| USOIL_EXNESS | 91.18 | 91.75 | 91.60 | 91.40 | **89.88** | **96.71** | 96.79 | **6.91** |
| XAUUSD_EXNESS | 57.73 | 57.70 | 57.35 | 56.80 | 55.73 | 60.32 | 59.40 | 4.59 |
| XAUUSD_ZS_EXNESS | 43.93 | 43.89 | 43.61 | 43.16 | 42.30 | 45.88 | 45.17 | 3.58 |
| BTCUSD_EXNESS | 51.82 | 51.86 | 51.85 | 51.84 | 51.88 | 51.89 | 51.35 | 0.54 |

Most symbols agree within ~1 point across every timeframe - strong evidence the underlying gap detection is sound and not a per-timeframe artifact. **USOIL_EXNESS is the one real outlier**: 1h coverage (89.88%) is noticeably lower than 4h/1d (96.7-96.8%), meaning USOIL_EXNESS's finer timeframes carry real, small, additional gaps that get absorbed/smoothed out once resampled to 4h or 1d. This is a genuine finding, not a bug - documented rather than silently averaged away. XAUUSD_EXNESS/XAUUSD_ZS_EXNESS show a smaller but real version of the same pattern (finer timeframes read slightly lower than 4h/1d). **Not yet root-caused** in this sprint (could be genuine low-liquidity micro-gaps at finer granularity, or a resampling-boundary artifact in how the source ticks were aggregated) - flagged as a Q1.2 follow-up in `Q1.1_COMPLETE.md`.

## Gap-aware execution boundary (Q1.1.12)

Per instruction, `execution_mtf.py` was **not modified**. Gap detection lives entirely at the API layer, computed *before* the engine ever runs:

```
POST /api/quant-lite/backtest
  -> validateBacktestRequest() -> checkDataCoverage() -> coveragePolicy.assessCoverage()   [pre-execution, real gap registry]
  -> if DATA_UNAVAILABLE: reject, no job created, no Python process spawned
  -> otherwise: job created with dataQuality attached, THEN executionAdapter spawns run_backtest_job.py
```

The API layer can and does safely detect gaps before execution - no engine-level gap awareness was found to be genuinely required for this sprint's scope. If a future need arises for the engine itself to *behave* differently around a gap (e.g. flatten a position before a closure, or refuse to compute an indicator across a gap boundary), that would be a separate, explicit engine change - not something Q1.1 needed or attempted.

## What "actual usable period" means

`CoverageAssessment.actual` is the real overlap between the requested range and the symbol's recorded `[minTs, maxTs]` - it is **not** clipped further around individual gaps inside that range (a request spanning a gap still shows `actual` = the full requested-and-recorded overlap, with the gap's effect visible instead through `coveragePct`/`largestGapDays`/`gapCountInRange`). This keeps "what you asked for" and "what actually exists at all" distinct from "how good is what exists" - conflating them would have made the RESTRICTED disclosure harder to read, not easier.
