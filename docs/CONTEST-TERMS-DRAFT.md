# AT24 Demo Trading Contest - Terms (DRAFT for lawyer review)

Status: DRAFT. Not public. The contest stays in DRAFT status in the system until counsel approves this text.

## 1. What this is
A free, skill-based demo-account contest run by AT24 (algotraders24.ai) for users of the free 30-day "XXX US30" demo EA. No purchase is needed to enter or to win. No real money is traded in the contest.

## 2. Rounds
- Round 1: 1 Nov 2026 00:00 to 30 Nov 2026 23:59 (broker server time of the entered account).
- Round 2: 1 Dec 2026 00:00 to 30 Dec 2026 23:59 (broker server time of the entered account).
(Dates may move if counsel or AT24 requires it; any change is published on the contest page before the round opens.)

## 3. Who can enter
- Adults (18+), or the age of majority where they live, with a registered AT24 account.
- Not available where contests of this kind are prohibited. [LAWYER: list excluded countries / age rules.]
- One entry per person per round. One demo trading account per entry.

## 4. How to enter
1. Start the free demo from /demo and install the demo EA on a DEMO account (real accounts are refused by the EA).
2. Connect that account with AT24 Live Sync so deals are reported.
3. Enter on the contest page, choosing a public alias (no real name or contact details in the alias).

## 5. Rules of play
- Only trades with magic number 33302 (the demo EA) count.
- Results are measured as profit % versus the balance at the start of the round.
- Risk limits: a daily loss above 5% or a total loss above 10% disqualifies the entry from the ranking.
- Minimum activity: 10 trading days and 20 trades in the round.
- No manual trades, other EAs, account resets, deposits/withdrawals that change the balance, or multiple accounts per person for the same round.

## 6. Ranking and prizes
- Eligible entries are ranked by profit % (highest first). Tie-break: smaller maximum loss, then more trades.
- Top 3 of each round each receive a 3-month licence for the full XXX US30 EA (Prize). Prizes have no cash value and are not transferable. One prize per person across both rounds.
- Every entrant who takes part (owner decision 2026-10-22: all who enter and participate, no performance condition) can claim a personal 20% discount link on the full product. Single use, tied to the entrant's account, valid until 14 days after the round ends, not combinable with other offers.

## 7. Data and verification
- Results are reported by the user's own trading terminal through AT24 Live Sync and are not verified by the broker. AT24 may exclude any entry it reasonably believes is manipulated, and its decision is final.
- By entering, the entrant agrees to the public leaderboard showing the alias, profit % and trade count.

## 8. Not an offer of financial services
The contest is on demo accounts only. Demo results do not predict live results, and nothing here is financial advice or a promise of profit. Trading involves risk of loss.
