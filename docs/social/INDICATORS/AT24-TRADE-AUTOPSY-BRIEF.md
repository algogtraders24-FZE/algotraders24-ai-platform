# AT24 Trade Autopsy - marketing brief (own product)

**Name:** AT24 Trade Autopsy  **Tagline:** Your trades, replayed: where did your exits leave money on the table?
**Platforms:** MT5 only for now (reads the account's closed trades of the chart symbol). **Type:** trade review tool; places no trades; not a signal.
**Assets:** `ea-research/indicators/AT24_Trade_Autopsy/branding/` (logo, screenshot_mt5.png; the screenshot uses labelled SAMPLE trades).

## What it solves
Most traders only see profit and loss. The Autopsy draws your closed trades on the chart and measures, on your own trades: how much of the best price the exit captured, how much heat winners took, how often price kept running after a winning exit, how often a loser came back to the entry, and the average result in R for trades that had a stop. It points at the leak (exits, stops, patience) with numbers instead of feelings. It pairs with the Edge Truth Test (is the result skill or luck) and the Position Size / Trade Planner tools.

## Honest claims you MAY make
- It measures the user's own closed trades against what price did on the user's own chart; the maths is verified against an independent Python calculation on 150 test trades.
- It is a review tool: it shows where exits and stops behaved badly, it does not tell the user what to do next and it places no trades.

## Claims you must NOT make
- No profit or win-rate promise; "price kept running" is hindsight, not proof that holding longer is better.
- Do not show the SAMPLE screenshot as real trading results.
- Not yet verified on a real account history (see the test record); do not claim that until one demo run is done.
