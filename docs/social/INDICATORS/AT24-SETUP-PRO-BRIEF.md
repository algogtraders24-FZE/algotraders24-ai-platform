# AT24 Setup Pro - marketing brief (own product, flagship)

**Name:** AT24 Setup Pro  **Tagline:** Setups with levels - and the receipts for every setup type on your own chart.
**Platforms:** MT5 only for now. **Type:** setup indicator + research scorecard; places no trades.
**Assets:** `ea-research/indicators/AT24_Setup_Pro/branding/` (logo, screenshot_mt5.png).

## What it solves
Most signal indicators show arrows and leave you to trust them. Setup Pro shows each setup with its entry, stop and target before the trade, a four-timeframe trend panel and alerts, and then scores every setup type on the symbol's own history: how many setups, hit rate, average result in R after cost, and whether that beats luck. When a setup type has shown no edge on your symbol, it says so.

## Honest claims you MAY make
- Non-repainting (closed bars only); levels shown before the trade; every setup is walked forward on the user's own bars and scored in R after cost with a luck test; the engine is verified against an independent Python calculation (343 test setups, all identical).
- On our XAUUSD H1 test feed both included setup types showed NO EDGE (average -0.05 R and -0.01 R): say that the tool is honest, not that the setups win.

## Claims you must NOT make
- No profit, win-rate or "accuracy" claims; no "signals"; a scorecard is in-sample history and past results do not predict future results.
- Do not describe the entry or stop logic in listing copy (generic wording only); first sentence 70-155 characters, title <= 40 characters.
- Live-setup block and alerts are not yet verified on a live chart (see the test record).
