# AT24 Market Heatmap - marketing brief (own product)

**Name:** AT24 Market Heatmap  **Tagline:** Forex, gold, oil, indices, stocks and crypto in one panel: what moved today, and what moved more than it should.
**Platforms:** MT5 only for now. **Type:** market overview; places no trades; not a signal.
**Assets:** `ea-research/indicators/AT24_Market_Heatmap/branding/` (logo, screenshot_mt5.png; retake the screenshot with broker symbols before use, mine includes the test symbol BTCUSD_T).

## What it solves
In fast markets (rate-hike bets, energy shocks, sharp equity selloffs) the question is which markets are moving abnormally right now. The Heatmap shows, for a watchlist of up to 25 symbols across all asset classes, the live move today, 1/5/21-day changes, how big the last daily move was in ATRs (with optional alerts for abnormal moves), trend, RSI and the position in the 52-week range, sortable by the biggest move.

## Honest claims you MAY make
- A descriptive multi-asset overview of the user's own broker symbols; the calculations are verified against an independent Python calculation on two symbols.
- It does not predict anything and places no trades.

## Claims you must NOT make
- No profit, win-rate or "signal" wording; an abnormal move is information, not a trade idea.
- Alerts are not yet verified on a live terminal and the 25-symbol load is not yet stress-tested (see the test record).
